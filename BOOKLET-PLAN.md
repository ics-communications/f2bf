# Free to be Faithful — 8-Page Promotional Booklet Plan

**Initiative:** Free to be Faithful (F2BF), a lifelong learning initiative of the Institute for Christian Studies (ICS)
**Format:** 8-page saddle-stitched booklet (2 folded sheets), portrait
**Date prepared:** 2026-05-11

---

## 1. Print Specifications

| Spec | Dimension |
|---|---|
| **Final trim size** | 5.5" × 8.5" (portrait) |
| **Bleed (all edges)** | 0.125" (1/8") |
| **Full bleed page size (artboard)** | 5.75" × 8.75" |
| **Safety / live area margin from trim** | 0.25" (0.375" from bleed edge) |
| **Live text area** | 5.0" × 8.0" |
| **Gutter (per page, inside edge)** | 0.375" minimum — text/critical art should not cross the spine |
| **Page count** | 8 (must be divisible by 4 for saddle-stitch) |
| **Imposition** | Reader spreads in InDesign; printer handles printer spreads |
| **Resolution** | 300 dpi at final size for raster art |
| **Color** | CMYK; convert RGB web assets before placing |
| **Black** | Body text 100K only; large display blocks may use rich black (60C 40M 40Y 100K) |
| **Fonts** | Embed or outline all type before export |
| **Export** | PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 with crop & bleed marks, 0.125" bleed |
| **Paper (recommended)** | Cover and text on 100lb matte text, or 80lb cover + 80lb text for a heavier feel; uncoated supports the editorial / contemplative tone |

## 3. Best Practices Research — Promotional Booklets

Synthesized from print-design and nonprofit communications conventions:

1. **One job per spread.** Each opening should answer one question the reader is asking ("What is this?" → "Why should I care?" → "What's happened?" → "How do I join?").
2. **Front cover sells the *idea*, not the catalog.** Avoid listing contents on the cover — use a single image, the wordmark, and a one-line promise.
3. **Inside front cover is the highest-value real estate after the cover.** Use it for the mission statement or a personal welcome, not a table of contents.
4. **Hierarchy of three.** On each page: one dominant element, one supporting element, one detail. More than three competing weights produces visual noise.
5. **Bleed discipline.** Any background color, photo, or graphic touching the edge must extend a full 0.125" past trim. Never place type or logos in the bleed zone.
6. **Safe-area discipline.** Phone numbers, URLs, dates, and small type must sit at least 0.25" inside trim — preferably 0.375" — because saddle-stitch trim wanders.
7. **Gutter awareness.** On a saddle-stitch 8-pager the binding crease is minimal, but never split a face, headline, or QR code across the spine.
8. **White space is a feature, not waste.** For a contemplative / academic audience, generous margins signal seriousness.
9. **Imagery: people > objects > abstractions.** Faces of community members or speakers outperform stock symbols. Use real photography from events when available.
10. **One CTA per spread, one *primary* CTA for the whole piece.** The reader should know what action the booklet is asking for (subscribe, register, donate, enroll).
11. **Mobile-friendly handoff.** Every printed CTA needs a frictionless digital follow-through: short URL, QR code, or both. QR codes must sit in safe area, minimum 0.75" square, with quiet zone.
12. **Accessibility.** Body text ≥ 10 pt; minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body type; avoid setting long text in reversed (light-on-dark) blocks.
13. **Proof and specificity.** Names, dates, places, and numbers earn trust. Vague aspirational copy loses it.
14. **Back cover is a destination, not a dump.** Treat the back cover as a second front cover — many readers see it first when handed the piece.
15. **Pacing.** Alternate dense and airy spreads to keep the reader moving.
16. **Print-test before press.** Print a folded mock-up on a desktop printer at 100% to check spread balance, gutter behavior, and small-type legibility.

## 4. Stipulations Honored

- **No in-depth course/event detail.** The timeline names each course/event with title, presenter, and date only — no descriptions, no registration prompts per item. Detail lives on the website; the booklet drives readers there.
- **Timeline from May 2025 onward** is included as its own spread (P4–P5).

## 5. Page-by-Page Plan

### P1 — Outside Front Cover

- **Image area:** Full-bleed contemplative photograph (e.g., the community-gathering hero image from the homepage), darkened with a teal-deep gradient overlay (~85% bottom → 10% top) for type legibility.
- **Top of page (in safe area):** Small all-caps eyebrow — "Institute for Christian Studies".
- **Lower third (anchored, in safe area):**
  - Wordmark: **Free *to be* Faithful** — display serif, cream, "to be" italic in slate-light.
  - Tagline beneath: *Equipping Christians for Democratic Life.*
  - Single thin red rule (matching the brand red `#C83C2C`).
  - Discrete year marker: **2025 – 2026 Season**.
- **No URL, no QR, no event list on the cover.** The cover sells the idea.
- **Bleed check:** photograph and gradient extend 0.125" past all edges.

### P2 — Inside Front Cover · Welcome / Mission

- **Eyebrow:** "Our Mission".
- **Headline (large display):** *Finding a Better Way.*
- **Body (3 short paragraphs, adapted from the site's About section):**
  1. Who F2BF is and who it's for.
  2. The "discipleship practice, not a political movement" framing.
  3. Invitation to read on.
- **Pull-quote / manifesto block (set apart, red rule above and below):**
  *Rooted without becoming rigid. Bold without becoming belligerent. Faithful without becoming fearful.*
- **Footer of page (small, in safe area):** Galatians 5:1 in italic, with reference — anchors the booklet scripturally without preaching.
- **No CTA here.** This page earns the reader's attention.

### P3 — The Three Pillars · Program · Resource · Community

(Faces P2; together they form the orientation spread.)

- **Heading:** *Program · Resource · Community.*
- **Sub:** *Three ways F2BF helps you connect faith and life.*
- **Three short stacked blocks** (numbered 01 / 02 / 03 in muted red, matching the website's pillar treatment). Each block has:
  - A one-word title.
  - A one-sentence description (≤ 18 words).
  - A three-item bulleted list (verb-led).
- Content mirrors the homepage's `f2bf-pillars` section but tightened.
- **No course names here** — pillars only. Course-level specifics are deferred to the timeline.
- **Small footer cue (safe area):** *Read on for what this past year has looked like.* → leads the reader into the timeline spread.

### P4–P5 — Spread · A Year of Free to be Faithful (Timeline, May 2025 → June 2026)

This is the **core editorial spread** and the only place dates and names appear.

- **Spread headline (crossing the gutter but not type — a thin red horizontal rule does cross):**
  *A Year of Free to be Faithful.*
- **Sub-headline:** *Where we've been since May 2025 — and where we're going next.*
- **Layout:** Vertical timeline running down the gutter side of each page (left page = 2025; right page = 2026). A single red rule connects the two sides across the gutter break. Each entry is a small dot on the rule + a short label set in the outer margin.
- **Entries (title + presenter only; no descriptions):**

  **2025 (left page, P4)**
  - **May 2025** — *Free to be Faithful*, Grand Rapids
  - **September 2025** — *Gender, Sexuality, and the Bible* · Keesmaat
  - **September 2025** — *Twentieth Century Authoritarianism* · Berglund
  - **September 2025** — *Fall Big Read* · Reichel and Du Mez
  - **October 2025** — *The Good, the True, the Beautiful* · Swallow Prior

  **2026 (right page, P5)**
  - **January 2026** — *Reading the Ruins* · Bick
  - **March 2026** — *The Christian-Jewish Question Today* · Taylor
  - **March 2026** — *The Evangelical Imagination* · Swallow Prior
  - **May 2026** — *Courageous Faith in a Time of Fear* (conference)
  - **June 2026** — *The Contemplative Life in the Age of Distraction* · Benjamins

- **Typographic treatment:** Months in small caps red; titles in display serif italic; presenters in body text small caps.
- **Visual texture:** A small icon system (e.g., dot = course, ring = conference) keeps the rhythm without naming categories explicitly.
- **Bleed check:** the red rule at the top/bottom of each page extends to bleed; the gutter region stays clear of type by ≥ 0.375".
- **No descriptive copy per entry, per stipulation #1.**

### P6 — *Courageous Faith in a Time of Fear* (Conference Teaser)

Light-touch spotlight — the timeline already names this. Here we give it visual prominence because it is the next major moment.

- **Eyebrow:** "May 13–14, 2026 · Toronto · In-person + livestream".
- **Display headline:** *Courageous Faith* / *in a Time of Fear* (matching homepage treatment).
- **One sub-paragraph (≤ 30 words)** stating it is a two-day public conference, co-presented with Knox College Ministry Forum — no agenda, no speaker bios.
- **Two small partner lockups** (Knox College Ministry Forum + ICS / F2BF), as on the homepage.
- **CTA:** *Full details and registration at f2bf.icscanada.edu* (plus QR code, 0.9" square, set in lower outer corner, inside safe area).
- This is the **first explicit CTA in the booklet** — by design.

### P7 — Inside Back Cover · Join the Movement

Primary acquisition page. This is where the booklet asks for the reader's action.

- **Eyebrow:** "Join the Movement".
- **Display headline:** *Embrace Your Freedom to Be Faithful.*
- **Body (2 short paragraphs):** Invitation to participate, framed in the booklet's voice ("discipleship practice," "common good").
- **Three numbered next-steps** (each one line, each with its own short URL or QR — but only **one** dominant QR for the *primary* action):
  1. **Explore courses** — f2bf.icscanada.edu/f2bf-courses
  2. **Subscribe on Substack** — instituteforchristianstudies.substack.com
  3. **Support ICS** — icscanada.edu/donate
- **Primary QR code** (≥ 1" square, with quiet zone) for `f2bf.icscanada.edu`, anchored bottom-right inside safe area.
- **Visual:** A muted secondary photograph (e.g., a reader, a discussion circle), heavily desaturated, framed at the top — not full-bleed, so the page reads as an action page, not an editorial page.

### P8 — Outside Back Cover · Contact & Provenance

The back cover is treated as a calm, business-card-like surface.

- **Top half:** ICS wordmark (dark on cream) + the F2BF wordmark, with a single red rule between them.
- **Contact block (safe area, ≥ 0.375" inside trim):**
  - 59 St. George Street, Toronto, Ontario M5S 2E6
  - 1-416-979-2331 · 1-888-326-5347 (toll-free)
  - info@icscanada.edu
  - **f2bf.icscanada.edu**
- **Footer line (small, italic):** *Free to be Faithful is a lifelong learning initiative of the Institute for Christian Studies, a graduate school in the Reformational tradition affiliated with the University of Toronto.*
- **Bottom-right:** Small social icon row (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Substack).
- **Copyright line, smallest type, lower-left safe area:** © 2026 Institute for Christian Studies.
- **No QR on the back cover** — the inside back cover owns the QR. The back cover stays clean.

## 6. Editorial / Pacing Map

| Spread | Pages | Density | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover | P1 | Airy | Hook |
| Mission spread | P2 ↔ P3 | Medium / Medium | Orient |
| Timeline spread | P4 ↔ P5 | Dense / Dense | Prove |
| Action spread | P6 ↔ P7 | Medium / Medium | Convert |
| Back | P8 | Airy | Close |

Pattern: airy → medium → dense → medium → airy. Reader is led in, immersed, and gently released.
