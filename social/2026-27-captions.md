## 1 · Spirals of Love and Healing

**Jim Olthuis · Tuesdays, September 15 – October 20, 2026**

### Primary caption

Healing is not a straight line. It spirals.

Over six Tuesday evenings this fall, Jim Olthuis — professor emeritus of philosophical theology and a practising psychotherapist for more than four decades — walks through the four turns of that spiral: letting-in, letting-go, letting-out, and letting-transform.

Trust and safety. Owning the pain and visiting it. Releasing, rebirthing. Re-integrating, re-connecting.

It is a course about what actually happens in the room when two people risk each other, and about the sacred space that opens up when love is allowed to do its slow work.

Six weeks. Fully online. Take it for your own sake, or for ICS credit.

## 2 · Keeping Faith Under Dictators

**Bruce Berglund · Wednesdays, September 16 – October 21, 2026**

### Primary caption

Christians have been here before.

Under fascist, Nazi, and communist regimes, European believers — Catholic and Protestant alike — had to work out what faithfulness costs. Some resisted. Some went along. Most lived somewhere in between, and had reasons.

Historian Bruce Berglund spends six Wednesday evenings on that record: what drew some Christians toward authoritarian politics, what held others back, and how ministers, artists, and intellectuals rebuilt their understanding of God, the church, and morality under pressure.

Not a history lesson for its own sake. A set of questions worth carrying into our own moment of Christian nationalism and hardening politics.

Six weeks. Fully online. Take it for your own sake, or for ICS credit.

## Posting notes

- **Pair them, don't merge them.** The tiles are built as a series — same skeleton, heads at
  identical coordinates — so they carry a two-image carousel or two consecutive posts well.
  Berglund's is the sharper hook for cold reach; Olthuis's converts better with people who
  already follow ICS.
- **Link in bio** is the assumed call to action, since the tiles carry no URL. Swap the phrase
  for the actual link on Facebook and LinkedIn, where links are live in the post body.
- **Price is deliberately off both the tile and the caption body.** $400 reads as friction in a
  feed; it belongs on the landing page, or in the first comment if someone asks.
- The Bonhoeffer hashtag is a reach play — he is not named in the course description. Drop it
  if you would rather not imply specific content.
