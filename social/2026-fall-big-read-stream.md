## YouTube title

Defying Tyrants: Matthew D. Taylor on Following Jesus in a World of Christian Antichrists | Fall Big Read

## YouTube description

When the word "antichrist" appears in the Bible, it is almost always plural, and it points
inward: at people inside the community who carry Christ's name while betraying his way.

Join Free to be Faithful for the Fall Big Read, an evening with Matthew D. Taylor on his new
book, Defying Tyrants: Following Jesus in a World of Christian Antichrists (Broadleaf Books,
2026). Taylor traces how Christian fixation with political power took hold, from the early
church and the Crusades through colonialism, slavery, and the Holocaust. He also follows the
Christians who resisted it: people who lived generous lives of sacrifice and stood up to
tyrants.

📅 Thursday, October 8, 2026 · 7:00–8:30 PM ET

ABOUT THE AUTHOR
Matthew D. Taylor is a scholar of Christian extremism and Senior Visiting Scholar at the
Center on Faith + Justice, Georgetown University. He is the author of The Violent Take It by
Force, and he taught The Christian–Jewish Question Today for Free to be Faithful in spring 2026.
