Our Mission

Finding a
Better Way.

Launched by the Institute for Christian Studies (ICS), Free to be Faithful is a program, a resource, and a community, designed to help Christians meet our complex moment.

Our courses, community, and events develop believers who are both faithfully Christian and deeply committed to the common good in our divided age.

Rooted without becoming rigid.
Bold without becoming belligerent.
Faithful without becoming fearful.
“For freedom Christ has set us free. Stand firm, therefore, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.” Galatians 5 : 1
What we offer

Program ·
Resource ·
Community.

Three ways F2BF helps you connect faith and life.
01

Program

Six-week online seminars led by world-class scholars, with the option to earn ICS credit toward degree completion.

  • Courses to foster deeper understanding
  • Retreats and intensives for all walks
  • Pathways toward a Master of Worldview Studies
02

Resource

Curated texts, essays, and dialogues on the issues facing Christians today, gathered, framed, and made shareable.

  • Curated texts to dive deeper
  • Shared expertise from scholars
  • Enriching dialogues to relive or discover
03

Community

Open events, coalitions, and conversations with welcoming people from every Christian tradition.

  • Open events to strengthen coalitions
  • Timely writing to challenge and inform
  • Welcoming people from every tradition
