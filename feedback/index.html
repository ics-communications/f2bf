Conference Feedback Questionnaire

Courageous Faith in a Time of Fear

Your reflections shape what comes next. Thank you for taking a few minutes to share them.

Thank you for participating in Courageous Faith in a Time of Fear. We are deeply grateful for your presence, insights, and engagement throughout the conference.

Your feedback will help us strengthen future gatherings and continue building spaces for thoughtful, courageous, and community-centered public faith. This survey should take approximately 5–8 minutes to complete, and all responses are anonymous.

Part 1

General Information

1. How did you participate in the conference?

2. Which best describes you?

3. How did you hear about the conference?

Part 2

Overall Experience

4. Please rate the following aspects of the conference.

Part 3

Conference Content & Impact

5. What was the most meaningful or impactful part of the conference for you?

6. Did the conference help you think differently about faith, fear, public life, or community formation? If so, how?

7. Were there particular speakers, sessions, or conversations that especially resonated with you? Why?

Part 4

Community & Conversation

One of the goals of this conference was not simply to present ideas, but to cultivate thoughtful and courageous community.

8. To what extent did the conference foster meaningful dialogue and connection?

9. Were there voices, perspectives, or topics you wished had been more present in the conference?

10. How well did the conference balance critique, hope, practical action, and theological reflection?

Part 5

Future Directions

11. What kinds of topics, themes, or questions would you like future conferences or Free to be Faithful events to explore?

12. Would you be interested in future opportunities connected to this work?

Thank you again for participating in Courageous Faith in a Time of Fear and for helping shape the future of these conversations through Free to be Faithful.
