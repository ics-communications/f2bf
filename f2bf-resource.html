02 — Reading

Curated Texts

Books, articles, and essays selected by the F2BF community and our scholars. These are the resources that have shaped our thinking and equipped us for the moment we're in.

On Tyranny: Twenty Lessons from the Twentieth Century

Timothy Snyder

A compact, urgent guide to recognising and resisting the patterns of authoritarianism, drawn from the darkest chapters of modern history.

For Such a Time as This: An Emergency Devotional

Hanna Reichel

A thoughtful framework for spiritual discernment amid political upheaval, rooted in scripture and the witness of the Confessing Church.

You Have a Calling: Finding Your Vocation in the True, Good, & Beautiful

Karen Swallow Prior

An invitation to rediscover the richness of Christian vocation in everyday life, guided by beauty, truth, and goodness.

Blessed are the Undone: Testimonies of the Quiet Deconstruction of Faith

Angela Reitsma Bick

An honest, compassionate look at what is happening inside Canadian churches and inside the hearts of those walking away.
