What to Expect

Free to be Faithful
Courses Involve…

Each course runs for six consecutive weeks, fully online, blending expert instruction with genuine community dialogue. Whether you're joining for personal enrichment or academic credit, you'll find a space built for honest inquiry.

Weekly Readings
Approximately 25 pages per week, accessible for a non-specialist audience.
Expert Instruction
Dynamic blend of lectures and in-class discussions with world-class scholars.
Live Zoom Seminars
Two-hour sessions once a week for six consecutive weeks.
Genuine Community
Engagement with enthusiastic participants from every tradition and walk of life.

Three Ways to Participate

Non-Credit

Take individual courses for your own interest and enrichment. No academic prerequisites required; just curiosity and a desire to grow.

For Credit

Take individual courses for ICS credit as you can fit them into your schedule. Build toward your degree at your own pace.

Full Degree

Complete the full program and earn a Master of Worldview Studies (MWS) degree from the Institute for Christian Studies.
